About these samples

Every number on the nine dashboards is invented. They are functional rebuilds of dashboards I designed and operate in production, reconstructed against synthetic datasets. I rebuilt them rather than copying and redacting the originals, because redaction is a process you can get almost right — regeneration is not.

The two storefront pages are the exception, and deliberately so. They replicate live public product and collection pages, so the product names, prices, attribute badges and photography are real public catalog content rather than invented. Nothing confidential sits on a public product page. They are static — no cart, no checkout, nothing transacts.

The production dashboards are additionally anonymized. Fake numbers are not sufficient there: the shape of a manufacturing dashboard reveals line configuration and staffing structure on its own. Those two carry a fictional brand and generic equipment names.

The marketing and analytics pages are shown under my employer's brand with their real structure, because that structure is ordinary industry practice and gives honest provenance for work I did there.

Happy to walk through the real versions, the warehouse schema underneath them, or the decisions behind any of it.
