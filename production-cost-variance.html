What's Driving the Overrun

A small number of SKUs carry most of the labor cost overrun, and the pattern is consistent: it is run size, not line speed, that drives it. Changeover and setup labor on a stage like Weigh & Fill or Tea Bag Machine is roughly fixed per run. Spread over 40,000 units it barely shows up in the per-unit cost; spread over 1,400 units — a small Sampler or a new-item Single-Serve batch — the same fixed labor becomes a large per-unit charge. The Sampler and low-volume Single-Serve lines above are not running inefficiently on the line; they are simply run in short batches, and the standard cost assumption baked into their pricing has not caught up with that. The monthly view shows the same effect at a smaller scale: the slower-volume months of the year, not just the smallest SKUs, show worse variance for the same reason.
