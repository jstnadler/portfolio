Answer it, don't dodge it

Why not the fifty-dollar one?

It sits still for years

Sleep mode holds charge up to 36 months. Works from −4°F to +140°F. A pack that went flat in your toolbox is worse than no pack.

It is sized for a truck

1,200A starting current. Rated to 8.5L gas, 6.5L diesel. The compressor reaches trailer pressure, not just car pressure.

Safe in someone else's hands

Keyed, colour-coded clamps resist reverse polarity. Protected against over-current, overheat, overcharge and shorts. You will lend this.

The price objection is the biggest thing between this product and a sale, and the live page never mentions it. Every claim in these three cards is BendPak's own spec.
Send the wrong buyer away

Right one for you?

Yes — if you tow or run a diesel

Truck, trailer, race car, side-by-side or boat, and you spend time where a tow truck is a two-hour problem. One box that starts the truck and fills a tire.

No — if it lives in a commuter car

You do not need 1,200 amps or trailer pressure. A smaller pack will do it for less. We would rather say so.

Disqualifying buyers builds more trust than claiming the product suits everyone. It also reframes the price: not an expensive small pack, a correctly priced big one.
