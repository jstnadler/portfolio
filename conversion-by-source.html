Orders by source over time

Platform-reported vs. first-party orders

Ad platforms count a conversion any time their own pixel or click model believes it influenced a sale, including view-through credit and cross-device modeling we can't independently verify. Our first-party numbers only count an order once, attributed to a single recorded session. The gap above is consistently one-sided -- platforms over-claim, never under-claim -- which is exactly why budget and channel-mix decisions run off the first-party numbers on this page, not the numbers in the ad accounts.
