Cumulative New Customers

Cumulative headcount of new customers acquired under the planned monthly budget, at the selected channel-mix scenario, over the chosen horizon. Efficiency drifts slightly as spend continues steadily (audience saturation), so monthly additions taper a little month over month.

CAC-Payback Curve

Cumulative contribution per customer, tracked from the month they are acquired. The dashed line is break-even (zero). Where the solid line first crosses it is the CAC-payback month shown above.

Planned Spend Allocation by Channel

Monthly budget split across four channel groups under the selected scenario. Prospecting and retargeting drive new-customer volume directly; retention/lifecycle spend mostly serves existing customers, so it dilutes blended new-customer efficiency the more of the budget it takes.
