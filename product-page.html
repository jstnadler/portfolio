      FAQ

      Is Teeccino real coffee?
      No. Teeccino is a caffeine-free herbal coffee alternative made from roasted chicory, carob, barley, dates and nuts, brewed the same way you'd brew ground coffee.
      Does French Roast contain caffeine?
      No, French Roast is 100% caffeine-free in every format.
      Is French Roast gluten-free?
      No. This blend contains barley and is not gluten-free. It also contains tree nuts (almonds).
      How should I store it?
      Keep the pouch sealed and store it in a cool, dry place after opening.
      Can I make it iced?
      Yes — brew it double-strength, then pour over ice.
